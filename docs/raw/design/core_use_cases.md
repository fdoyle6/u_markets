## Topics of Interest

Before creating the big groups of questions, outlining the potential questions of interest are useful. Below is the general list of questions that the author believes are within scope and of interest to study:

1. Constrained Supply vs Demand - What shape do the supply vs demand curves actually take in different environments & economies?
2. Organizational Efficiency vs Architecture - How does organizational efficiency vary with the architecture of the organization?
3. Executive Paycurve - How much value do executives add to organizations?
4. Employee Raise Structure - How should employee raises be structured to both drive organizational growth and maximize employee happiness?
5. Organizational Turnover - How much turnover is optimal for a given business to ensure that they get and keep the best (and not the worst) people in a constrained organization?
6. Trust Conditions - What are the conditions where a market can become a trust (i.e. when the producers can ignore the desires of the consumers)?
7. Sustainable Market Size - How large can a given market get given a different consumer availability?
8. Micro-Business Economy - How sustainable are economies that do not have middlemen and how more expensive does that make products for the consumer?
9. Total Market Value - How is the total value in a market constrained?
10. Free Market Freedom - Under what conditions are free markets self-sustaining?
11. Government Interactions - How do companies and markets interact to changes from the government.
    * This is admittedly vague and should be refined but the company-government/regularitory body interactions are of interest. This will be refined later.

Of note, these different conditions may be tackled piece-by-piece or as part of a study set. There also may be incremental sub-studies that will be used to build out capabilites.
